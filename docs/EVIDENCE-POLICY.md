# Evidence policy

ShockAudit distinguishes policy identity, documentary support, model interpretation, and researcher judgment. Confirming an event does not verify every claim about it. Selecting a design does not establish exogeneity or causal validity.

## Source support

| Material | Permitted interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Exact official record field | Support for the corresponding identity or administrative fact. |
| Retained official document passage | Support for the claim actually stated in the passage, within its version and scope. |
| Official guidance or explanatory material | Attributed explanation, with its status distinguished from controlling legal text. |
| Scholarly or contextual metadata | A discovery lead unless relevant source text has also been inspected. |
| Model assessment | A conditional interpretation or hypothesis subject to validation and researcher review. |

Search titles, snippets, source counts, and accessible URLs do not by themselves establish coverage, treatment timing, enforcement, exposure, or a mechanism. Distinguish publication, announcement, adoption, legal effect, implementation, compliance, amendment, and repeal. An uncertain or partial date must remain uncertain or partial.

## Retrieval and conflicts

Retain the source identity, locator, retrieval status, role, and available text. Keep different policy versions and episodes distinct. Inaccessible documents, failed routes, conflicting claims, and incomplete coverage remain limitations. Do not fill those gaps with a similar policy or an unsupported date.

Confirmation controls depend on the relevant contract's active source support. An unresolved conflict or stale input must not become a confirmed fact through repeated clicks or fluent prose.

## Scientific interpretation

Design advice identifies assumptions and checks that the researcher must examine. Concurrent events are possibilities for review rather than automatically valid controls. A proposed mechanism or directional hypothesis is not an observed process or estimated effect.

The current mechanism workflow provides model-suggested scholarly citations as leads for independent checking. A citation to an intermediate proposition does not verify the complete pathway or its transfer to the study. Do not report literature as inspected when only a citation was suggested.

Evidence collection and preliminary qualification remain outcome-blind. Exported plans must distinguish proposed diagnostics from analyses actually performed.
